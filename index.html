Mello Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Mello is a personal finance app designed to help users track expenses, manage bills, and understand spending patterns in a simple and private way.

1. Information We Collect

Mello does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal identity information.

The app may store information entered by the user, such as expenses, bills, income amount, username, and currency preference, locally on the user's device.

2. How Information Is Used

Information entered into Mello is used only to provide app features, including expense tracking, bill tracking, financial health overview, and spending insights.

3. Data Storage

Mello stores user data locally on the user's device. We do not operate a cloud database for storing user financial data in the current version of the app.

4. Data Sharing

Mello does not sell, rent, or share user financial data with third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Mello may use platform services provided by Google Play for app distribution and basic app functionality. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

Mello is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Deletion

Since user data is stored locally on the device, users can delete their data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
